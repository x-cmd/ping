# About this page

A ping from your laptop tells you one thing: how far away *your* network is from the target. It says nothing about whether the target is reachable from Lagos, Osaka, or São Paulo.

Measuring from many vantage points at once answers the question people actually have — **"is it just me, or is it everyone?"**

That is what [Globalping](https://globalping.io) is: a network of volunteer probes in datacenters and eyeball networks worldwide that run the measurement on your behalf and hand back structured results — per probe, with the round-trip times, the packet loss, and the coordinates.

Here are measurements taken against `1.1.1.1` on **2026-10-29**, across **18 probes in 14 countries on 6 continents**, 4 packets each. One probe (netcup, Nuremberg) had 100% packet loss and is excluded from the averages.

> CLI: `x ping` — see [x-cmd.com/mod/ping](https://x-cmd.com/mod/ping) · `x globalping` (the official CLI, packaged by x-cmd)
> Data: [api.globalping.io](https://api.globalping.io/v1/probes)
